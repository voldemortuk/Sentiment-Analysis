The challenge today
Your employee voice data exists. It just doesn't connect.
How it works now
People Forum — employees type concerns into a system. Separate from everything else.
Town Hall Q&A — submitted questions + live questions, captured differently by every note-taker, in every office.
Live sessions — audio from People Forum sessions, photos of whiteboards, handwritten notes, Excel logs. No standard format. Different note-takers across offices.
Manual consolidation — someone spends 1–2 weeks pulling this together before insights reach the CHRO.
Result: By the time it reaches leadership, the moment has passed.
What's possible
One pipeline — every source feeds into the same system, regardless of format.
AI reads everything — typed text, session audio, even photos of whiteboards. All normalised automatically.
Instant classification — every concern gets a sentiment, a theme, and an urgency score in seconds.
Real-time dashboard — the CHRO sees what the workforce feels today, not what they felt 2 weeks ago.
Result: Act while the conversation is still warm.
Input flexibility

Your options
Three scopes. You choose the depth.
Option A
Excel-to-Dashboard
Start with the data you already have. Import People Forum + Town Hall Q&A from spreadsheets. AI classifies. Dashboard surfaces insights.
Best for: Quick proof of value from existing data.
Inputs: Typed text (Forum + Q&A submissions)
Option B — Recommended
Full Voice Capture
Everything in Option A, plus AI transcription from live session audio and vision reading from whiteboard photos. No concern left behind.
Best for: Capturing the full picture — especially the live, unfiltered voice that gets lost today.
Inputs: Text + session audio + whiteboard images
Option C
Full Voice + Action Tracking
Everything in Option B, plus an action queue where high-urgency concerns get assigned to HR owners, tracked to resolution, and reported to the CHRO.
Best for: Closing the loop — not just hearing the voice, but proving action was taken.
Inputs: Text + audio + images + action workflows
