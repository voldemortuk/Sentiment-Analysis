The challenge today
Your employee voice data exists. It just doesn't connect.
HOW IT WORKS NOW
People Forum
e&’s primary employee voice event. Once a year, in person, across every office — Dubai HQ, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Cairo, Riyadh. 100–150 employees per office, 1-week event.
During the Forum
Audio, whiteboards, handwritten notes, Excel logs. Different note-takers per office = inconsistent formats.
After the Forum
Written feedback arrives separately, in different formats, from different offices.
No cross-office visibility
Each office’s data lives in its own silo. No way to compare sentiment or themes across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Cairo, or Riyadh in one view.
No trend detection
Same concerns may surface year after year with no easy way to prove whether previous actions made a difference.
Manual consolidation
1–2 weeks pulling it all together across offices. No way to spot themes early.
Manual resolution
Every follow-up, assignment, and status update done manually. No action tracker. No progress reporting. Concerns raised but never tracked to closure.
Insights arrive weeks after the conversation — making it harder to act in time.
